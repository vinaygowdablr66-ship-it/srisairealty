Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

1. Introduction

Sri Sai Realty ("we", "us", "our") is a property consulting and management firm based in Bengaluru, India. We operate the website srisairealty.in and related pages.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. By using our website or submitting an enquiry, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We are committed to handling your personal data responsibly and in accordance with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDPA"), to the extent in force.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us through our enquiry form and other interactions. This includes:

  • Contact details: your first and last name, email address, and phone number;
  • Property preferences: your interest (buying, selling, renting, investment, commercial, management), budget range, and preferred location;
  • Message content: anything you write in the enquiry message field;
  • Communication preferences: whether you have opted in to receive updates on WhatsApp;
  • Property context: details of any specific property listing you enquire about.

We may also collect basic technical usage data (such as browser type and pages visited) through standard analytics tools to improve our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to your enquiries and providing the services you request;
  • Sharing property listings and information relevant to your stated interest, budget, and location;
  • Sending updates you have opted into via WhatsApp or other agreed channels;
  • Improving our website content, services, and customer experience;
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Storage and Security

Enquiry data is stored securely in a cloud database (Supabase/PostgreSQL) with access limited only to authorised administrators of Sri Sai Realty through a password-protected admin panel.

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures — including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and limited retention — to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, to respond to you, and to comply with legal and accounting requirements. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it.

If you would like your enquiry data erased, you may contact us as described in Section 8 and we will act on your request within applicable legal timelines.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not disclose your personal information to third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • To service providers (such as cloud infrastructure providers) who help us operate our website and store data;
  • To property owners, developers, or partners when necessary to respond to an enquiry you have made about a specific property;
  • Where required by law, court order, or government authority;
  • With your explicit consent.

Third-party service providers are expected to handle your data only in accordance with our instructions and applicable law.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site, remember preferences, and improve performance. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some features of the site.

Where third-party analytics or advertising services are used, their own privacy policies apply to their collection and use of data.

8. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Erase your information, subject to legal requirements;
  • Withdraw consent for communications at any time;
  • Complain to the relevant authority if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, you can contact us using the details in Section 9. We will respond within 30 days unless a longer period is required by law.
